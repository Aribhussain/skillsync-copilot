# Problem Statement 26242

**AI-Assisted Skill Assessment Tool for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)**
Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship · Theme: Smart Education · Category: Software · Smart India Hackathon 2026

## Context

Most of India's informal workforce learned their trade on the job and has no credential. Over 91% of India's employment is informal. NCVET's **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)** certifies these workers against **NSQF** levels. Today RPL assessment is:

- **manual** and slow to scale,
- **inconsistent** across assessors and locations,
- **hard to schedule** for workers who can't take time off.

Integrity is a real problem as well. The **CAG Performance Audit Report No. 20 of 2025 on PMKVY** found the **same photographs reused as assessment evidence across states**.

## What the PS asks for

An AI-assisted RPL tool that:

1. Guides a worker through a **structured self-declaration** of prior experience, **auto-mapped to the closest NSQF qualification pack(s)**.
2. Supports **practical skill evaluation** with guided task checklists and, where feasible, **image/video assessment aids** that help assessors score consistently.
3. **Standardises scoring rubrics** across assessors and locations to reduce evaluator-to-evaluator variance.
4. Generates an **NSQF-aligned competency profile and a certification recommendation for assessor sign-off**. It supports the human assessor and never replaces them.
5. Works in **low-connectivity settings**, with offline capture and later sync.

## Expected outcomes

- A working workflow from self-declaration to the assessor scoring interface, for at least one trade.
- An NSQF QP mapping engine.
- Evidence of consistency improvement (inter-assessor agreement).
- An offline-capable mobile/web interface.
- A clear statement of where the tool supports and where it replaces assessor judgement.
